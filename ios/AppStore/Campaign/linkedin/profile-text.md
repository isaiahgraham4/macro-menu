## Experience entry

Title: Founder and iOS Developer
Company: Leanrrr (self-employed)
Location: Melbourne, Victoria, Australia

Description:

I design, build and run Leanrrr, an iPhone calorie tracker for people who eat out. Most food diaries treat takeaway as an afterthought. Leanrrr starts from it. Tell it where you are and it builds and ranks whole orders that fit the calories, protein and budget you have left for the day. It covers nearly 20 Australian chains plus menus from the US, Canada, the UK and New Zealand.

The nutrition figures come from each chain's own published data, not crowd-sourced entries. Where a number is an estimate the app says so, and where there's no number it leaves the gap instead of filling in a zero.

I've done all of it on my own: the SwiftUI app, widgets and Live Activities, Apple Health and iCloud sync, a menu scanner that runs on the phone, the data pipelines that collect each chain's menu, the subscription tier, the App Store listing and the marketing.

Skills: Swift, SwiftUI, iOS development, product design, data pipelines, App Store Connect

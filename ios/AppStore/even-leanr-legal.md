## Terms.md: replace the "Cost" section with

## Even Leanr, purchases and refunds

Leanr is free to use. Even Leanr is an optional subscription that adds custom app colours and icons, separate macro colours, the Calories & macros and All targets widgets, and choosing your meal reminders. Logging, searching, scanning, photos, Siri and your data stay free.

- It's sold through Apple's App Store, which handles payment. In Australia it's A$5.99 a month or A$49.99 a year. The price for your country and the billing period are shown before you buy, and there are no other fees.
- New subscribers get a 7-day free trial. Apple allows one trial per Apple Account. You aren't charged during the trial. When it ends, your subscription starts and the price shown at sign-up is charged to your Apple Account, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. A cancelled trial stops at the end of its 7 days.
- It renews automatically until you cancel it in your Apple Account settings, at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Cancelling keeps Even Leanr until the end of the period you've paid for.
- Apple may change the price at renewal and will tell you first. You can cancel before it takes effect.
- Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
- If Even Leanr ends, the extras go back to their defaults. Everything you've logged and saved stays.
- It's tied to your Apple Account. "Restore purchases" brings it back on a new iPhone.

Nothing in these terms limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law. That includes a refund or remedy when something has a major fault.

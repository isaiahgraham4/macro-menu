## Not medical advice

Leanrrr gives general nutrition information to help you choose and log food. It isn't medical, dietary or health advice, and targets are estimates. Talk to a doctor or Accredited Practising Dietitian before changing how you eat, especially if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, under 18, have a medical condition, or have a history of disordered eating.

## Nutrition and allergens

- Restaurant figures come from each chain's own published nutrition information on the date shown in Settings → Menu sources. Chains change recipes, and figures vary between stores and serves. Leanrrr downloads updated menus without an app update, and the date shown is the menu in use.
- Custom orders (with ingredients taken off) and recipes are estimates.
- Everyday foods (such as bananas and chicken breast) are average figures from the U.S. Department of Agriculture's FoodData Central. Brands, varieties and cooking change them.
- Scanned and searched packaged products, and the supermarket products in Find, come from Open Food Facts, which is edited by volunteers. Check them against the package.
- When you log from a photo, your {{DEVICE}} only names the foods, and it can't count them or read portions. The figures are Leanrrr's own, but the match and the amount are up to you, so check both before logging.
- Leanrrr isn't an allergen guide. Always check allergens with the restaurant.

You can choose to share foods you've added with other Leanrrr users. When you share one:

- Only share figures from the product's label, the restaurant or another source you trust, and only for a real food.
- Don't put offensive, abusive, sexual or hateful words, advertising, links or contact details in a shared food.
- Don't share anything illegal, or anything that promotes eating disorders, self-harm or risky weight-loss products.
- You let Leanrrr show that food to other users, for free and for as long as it's shared. It stays yours, and you can take it down at any time.

There's no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Any food can be reported from its page, and anyone's shared foods can be hidden. Illegal content is removed, and may be reported to the police.

Foods other people share haven't been checked by me. Compare them with the label before relying on them.

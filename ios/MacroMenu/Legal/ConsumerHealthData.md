# Leanrrr consumer health data privacy policy

Effective 22 November 2026

This policy is for Washington's My Health My Data Act, Nevada's consumer health data law and similar US state laws, and it applies to everyone who uses Leanrrr. It sits beside the main privacy policy, which has the full detail. Leanrrr is made by Isaiah Graham, an independent developer in Victoria, Australia. You can reach me at {{CONTACT}}.

## The short version

- Leanrrr works with health information you enter, such as what you eat, your weight and your goals, to show your totals, trends and targets.
- It does that on your {{DEVICE}}. I never receive your food log, weight, measurements, photos or targets.
- Leanrrr never sells consumer health data, never uses it for advertising and never shares it with anyone except where you direct it, as described below.

## Consumer health data Leanrrr handles, and why

All of these are kept on your {{DEVICE}}, in your own iCloud if sync is on, and in Apple Health only if you connect it.

- **Food and drink you log**, with their energy, protein, carbs, fat and other nutrients: your daily totals, history, insights, reminders and exports.
- **Weight, body measurements, water and progress photos:** your trends, check-ins and progress screens.
- **Age, height, sex, activity level and weight goal:** estimating your energy and protein targets.
- **Dietary needs and allergens you choose:** leaving out menu items that don't fit.
- **Steps and active energy read from Apple Health:** showing steps and calories burnt, and adjusting your target.

Leanrrr only uses this data to give you the features you ask for. It's never used to infer a health condition, and it's never used for anything else.

## Where it comes from

- From you: what you type, say, scan or choose in Leanrrr.
- From Apple Health, if you connect it and allow each type.
- From your camera or photo library, read on your {{DEVICE}} to recognise food or labels. Meal photos aren't kept.

## What is shared, and with whom

- **Apple iCloud (your private iCloud Drive):** a copy of your log, foods, targets, water, measurements and the weigh-ins you enter, only while iCloud sync is on. Apple stores it under your Apple Account; I can't see it.
- **Apple Health:** the food, water and weights you choose to write, only if you connect Apple Health and allow it.
- **Other Leanrrr users, through Apple's iCloud public database:** one food's name, serving, nutrition, category and barcode, with an anonymous ID, only for a food you choose to share. Nothing about you or your log is included.
- **Open Food Facts (France):** a product's barcode number, or the words you search for packaged foods, only when you scan or search. Search words could hint at a diet, so nothing linking them to you is sent.

No one else receives consumer health data. Leanrrr has no affiliates. RevenueCat, which manages Even Leanrrr purchases, receives purchase records only, never health data.

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- Confirm whether Leanrrr collects, shares or sells your consumer health data, and see it
- Get a list of everyone it has been shared with, and how to contact them
- Withdraw your consent
- Have it deleted

Because your data stays on your {{DEVICE}}, you can do most of this yourself:

- **See it:** Meal → My foods → Export all app data, or export your food log or daily totals as a CSV file.
- **Withdraw consent:** turn off iCloud sync or disconnect Apple Health in Settings, or turn off sharing for a food. You can also remove Leanrrr's Health access in the Health app.
- **Delete it:** Settings → Delete all my data removes everything on this {{DEVICE}} and in its iCloud copy and takes down any foods you shared. It can also remove what Leanrrr wrote to Apple Health.

You can also email {{CONTACT}} with any request. I'll answer within 45 days, free of charge, up to twice a year. If I need more time, I'll tell you why within those 45 days, and the most it can take is 90 days in all. If I delete something at your request, I'll also ask anyone who received it from Leanrrr to delete it.

## Appeals

If I turn down your request, you can appeal by emailing {{CONTACT}} with "Appeal" in the subject. I'll reply within 45 days with my decision and the reasons. If your appeal is turned down, you can contact the Washington State Attorney General at atg.wa.gov/file-complaint, or your own state's attorney general.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app with a new effective date. Leanrrr won't start collecting or sharing consumer health data in a new way without asking you first.

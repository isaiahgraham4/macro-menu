# Leanr privacy policy

Effective 5 October 2026

Leanr is made by Isaiah Graham, an independent developer in Victoria, Australia ("I", "me"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Leanr. You can reach me at {{CONTACT}}.

## The short version

- Leanr has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It doesn't use cookies.
- Everything you enter stays on your device and, if you choose, in your own iCloud and Apple Health. I never receive it.
- A few features ask other services for information. They are told only what they need: a barcode number, a search, or the positions of restaurants.

## What Leanr keeps on your device

Your foods, recipes, saved meals, food log, weigh-ins, targets, reminders and settings are saved on your iPhone, inside Leanr's own storage. Your target estimate uses your age, height, weight, sex and activity level. Those figures are only used for the calculation on your device.

## iCloud

iCloud sync is on unless you turn it off in Settings. It keeps a copy of your foods, log, targets and the weigh-ins you enter in Leanr's folder in your private iCloud Drive, so your other devices can use it. Weights read from Apple Health are never copied to iCloud. Apple stores that copy under your Apple Account and I can't see it.

## Apple Health

Leanr only uses Apple Health if you connect it in Settings, and you choose which types it may read or write. If you connect:

- Leanr writes the food you log and the weights you enter.
- Leanr reads your weight and active energy to show your trend and estimate your target.

Health information is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never leaves your device except through Apple's own Health sync.

## Camera

The camera is used to scan barcodes and read nutrition labels. This happens on your device. Images aren't saved or sent anywhere.

## Location

If you allow it, Leanr uses your location only while you're using the app, to find nearby restaurants.

- Apple Maps receives your position to search for nearby restaurants (Apple's privacy policy applies).
- To show opening hours, Leanr sends the positions of nearby restaurants to the OpenStreetMap Overpass API, run by FOSSGIS e.V. in Germany. Your own position isn't sent, but the restaurants' positions show roughly where you are. Answers are kept on your device for up to a week.

You can turn location off at any time in the Settings app. Leanr works without it.

## Barcode lookups

When you scan a product, its barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts (a non-profit in France) to look up its nutrition. Nothing else is sent.

## Links to other apps and websites

Buttons such as "Open MyMacca's", the delivery apps and "View source" open other apps or websites. Only the restaurant's name is passed to delivery apps. Their own privacy policies apply once you're there.

## Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device and only check your log on your device. Turn each one off in Settings, or turn off all of Leanr's notifications in the Settings app.

## Children

Leanr isn't designed for children under 13, and the target estimate is only for adults 18 and over. Leanr doesn't collect personal information from anyone. If you're under 18, use Leanr with a parent's or doctor's guidance.

## Deleting your data

- To delete one item, swipe it or tap it and choose Delete.
- Settings → Delete all my data removes everything Leanr keeps on this device and its iCloud copy. It can also remove the food and weights Leanr saved to Apple Health.
- Deleting the app removes what's on the device.

Because I don't hold any of your data, there's nothing for me to delete on my side. If you have a question, contact me and I'll answer within 30 days.

## Overseas services

Apple (United States and elsewhere), Open Food Facts (France) and the OpenStreetMap Overpass API (Germany) receive the information described above when you use those features.

## Security

Your data is protected by your device's encryption and your Apple Account. Leanr only connects to other services over HTTPS.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app with a new effective date.

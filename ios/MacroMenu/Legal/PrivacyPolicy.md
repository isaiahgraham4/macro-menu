# Leanrrr privacy policy

Effective 26 November 2026

Leanrrr is made by Isaiah Graham, an independent developer in Victoria, Australia ("I", "me"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Leanrrr. You can reach me at {{CONTACT}}.

## The short version

- Leanrrr has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It doesn't use cookies. If you buy Even Leanrrr, a record of that purchase goes to RevenueCat, which manages subscriptions for me (see Purchases).
- Everything you enter stays on your device and, if you choose, in your own iCloud and Apple Health. I never receive it.
- The exceptions are a food you choose to share with other Leanrrr users, and a suggestion you choose to send me. Only that food's name, serving, nutrition and barcode are shared, and only the suggestion you type is sent, without your name.
- A few features ask other services for information. They are told only what they need: a barcode number, the words you search for packaged foods, your position for an Apple Maps search, or the address of a recipe page you ask Leanrrr to load.

## What Leanrrr keeps on your device

Your foods, recipes, saved meals, food log, meal plans, weekly check-ins, weigh-ins, water, body measurements, progress photos, targets, reminders and settings are saved on your {{DEVICE}}, inside Leanrrr's own storage. Your target estimate uses your age, height, weight, sex and activity level. Those figures are only used for the calculation on your device.

## iCloud

iCloud sync is on unless you turn it off in Settings. It keeps a copy of your foods, log, meal plans, check-ins, targets, water, body measurements and the weigh-ins you enter in Leanrrr's folder in your private iCloud Drive, so your other devices can use it. Weights read from Apple Health are never copied to iCloud, and neither are progress photos. Apple stores that copy under your Apple Account and I can't see it.

## Apple Health

Leanrrr only uses Apple Health if you connect it in Settings, and you choose which types it may read or write. If you connect:

- Leanrrr writes the food you log, the water you log (one total for each day) and the weights you enter.
- Leanrrr reads your weight, active energy (your calories burnt) and steps to show your trend, your steps and your calories burnt, and to estimate your target. Step counts and calories burnt are held in memory while you use Leanrrr, and the last week's figures are shared with Leanrrr's Steps and Calories burnt widgets through a file on this {{DEVICE}}. If you choose one source for steps or calories burnt, Leanrrr remembers which one on this {{DEVICE}}. These figures are never saved in Leanrrr's data, its iCloud copy or its exports.

Health information is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never leaves your device except through Apple's own Health sync.

## Camera

The camera is used to scan barcodes, read nutrition labels and restaurant menus, recognise the foods in a photo of your meal and take progress photos. Recognising food happens on your device, using Apple Intelligence where your {{DEVICE}} has it and Apple's built-in image recognition otherwise. Photos of meals aren't saved or sent anywhere, and you can also choose a photo from your library instead; Leanrrr only sees the one you pick.

## Describing a meal

If you type or say what you ate, your {{DEVICE}} reads the words to find each food, using Apple Intelligence where your {{DEVICE}} has it and Leanrrr's own reading otherwise. Both happen on your device. The words aren't saved or sent anywhere; only the foods you choose to log are kept. Saying it uses your keyboard's dictation, which Apple's privacy policy covers.

## Asking Leanrrr and dietary needs

If you ask Leanrrr a question on Find, such as "40 g protein under 600 Cal at Nando's", your {{DEVICE}} reads it to set up an order search, using Apple Intelligence where your {{DEVICE}} has it and Leanrrr's own reading otherwise. Both happen on your device, and the question isn't saved or sent anywhere. The dietary needs you choose (such as vegetarian, no pork or an allergen to avoid) are kept only on this {{DEVICE}} and are used only to leave out menu items that don't fit.

## Recipe links

If you import a recipe from a link, Leanrrr loads that page straight from the website, as a browser would, and reads only the recipe's name, servings, ingredients and nutrition. Nothing about you or your data is sent with the request. Like any website, the site sees your device's IP address and that the page was requested, and its own privacy policy applies. The page itself isn't kept; only the recipe you save is.

## Apple Watch

If you install Leanrrr on Apple Watch, your iPhone sends it today's calories, protein and water against your targets, today's calories burnt if Apple Health is connected, and the meals and foods you can log from the watch. They go directly between your iPhone and your paired watch through Apple's own connection, never through a server, and the watch keeps only the latest copy. Anything you log on the watch is sent back to your iPhone and saved there like anything else you log.

## Progress photos

If you add progress photos, they're kept as files inside Leanrrr's storage on this {{DEVICE}}. They are never uploaded, never part of the iCloud copy, backups or exports Leanrrr makes, and never used for anything but showing them to you. Your {{DEVICE}}'s own backup may include them, like any app's files. Delete a photo in Leanrrr to remove it, or use Settings → Delete all my data to remove them all.

## Siri and Visual Intelligence

You can ask Siri to look up a food or log a meal in Leanrrr. If you use Visual Intelligence (pointing your camera at food, or a screenshot), Apple's system tells Leanrrr what it sees on your device so Leanrrr can show matching foods from its own lists. Leanrrr doesn't keep what it's given, and doesn't send it anywhere. Apple's privacy policy covers Siri and Visual Intelligence themselves.

## Location

If you allow it, Leanrrr uses your location only while you're using the app, to find nearby restaurants. Apple Maps receives your position to search for them, and opening hours are shown in Apple Maps' own place card. Apple's privacy policy applies to both.

You can turn location off at any time in the Settings app. Leanrrr works without it, and you can search for a suburb or address instead.

## Foods you share

Sharing is off for every food unless you turn it on for that food. When you do, its name, serving size, nutrition figures, category and barcode are saved in Leanrrr's public database in Apple's iCloud, where anyone using Leanrrr can see them. Your name, Apple Account, log and other foods are never part of it. iCloud attaches an anonymous ID that's only used inside Leanrrr, so you can change or remove what you shared and other people can hide everything from one sharer. I can't use it to work out who you are.

Turning sharing off for a food, or deleting the food, takes the shared copy down. Settings → Delete all my data takes down everything you shared. Foods that break the terms of use may be removed.

If you report a shared food, the report records which food it was, the reason you chose and your anonymous ID, so each person's report counts once. Foods other people shared are downloaded to your {{DEVICE}} so search works offline. Turn them off in Settings → Shared foods.

## Suggestions

When you send a suggestion from Settings → Suggestions, I receive what you typed, the kind of suggestion you picked, your app version and which country's restaurant menus you use. It's stored in Leanrrr's database on Supabase (see Other services) with the time it was sent. It doesn't include your name, an account, a device ID or anything you log, so I can't reply to it or tell who sent it. Please don't put personal details in a suggestion. I keep suggestions while they're useful for improving Leanrrr and delete them after that.

## Product lookups

When you scan a product, its barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts (a non-profit in France) and to Leanrrr's food database on Supabase, which holds the US government's (USDA) list of packaged-food labels, to look up its nutrition. Nothing else is sent with it.

When you tap "Search packaged foods", the words you typed are sent to Open Food Facts to find matching products, and opening a result sends that product's barcode number. Nothing is sent while you type, and nothing else is sent with your search.

## Links to other apps and websites

Buttons such as "Open MyMacca's", the delivery apps and "View source" open other apps or websites. Only the restaurant's name is passed to delivery apps. Their own privacy policies apply once you're there.

## Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device and only check your log on your device. Turn each one off in Settings, or turn off all of Leanrrr's notifications in the Settings app.

## Children

Leanrrr isn't designed for children under 13, and the target estimate is only for adults 18 and over. The only thing Leanrrr collects from anyone is a food they choose to share, as described under Foods you share. If you're under 18, use Leanrrr with a parent's or doctor's guidance.

## Age range

Where the law requires apps to check age, such as for new Apple Accounts in Texas, Leanrrr asks the App Store for your age range: under 13, 13 to 15, 16 to 17, or 18 and over. Apple decides what to share, and you can see it in your Apple Account settings. Leanrrr keeps the range only on this {{DEVICE}}, uses it only to apply its minimum age of 13, and never sends it anywhere. Nowhere else does Leanrrr ask.

## Deleting your data

- To delete one item, swipe it or tap it and choose Delete.
- Settings → Delete all my data removes everything Leanrrr keeps on this device and its iCloud copy, and takes down any foods you shared. It can also remove the food, water and weights Leanrrr saved to Apple Health.
- Deleting the app removes what's on the device.

Apart from foods you chose to share and suggestions you sent (which aren't linked to you), I don't hold any of your data, so there's nothing for me to delete on my side. If a food you shared can't be taken down from the app, contact me and I'll remove it. If you have a question, contact me and I'll answer within 30 days.

## Purchases

Even Leanrrr is bought through Apple. Apple handles your payment, including the free trial, and tells Leanrrr, on your device, whether your subscription or trial is active. I don't receive your name, your Apple Account or any payment details, and there's no Leanrrr account.

To keep track of subscriptions, Leanrrr sends a record of each Even Leanrrr purchase, renewal, trial and restore to RevenueCat. The record holds the plan, price, currency, dates, Apple's transaction ID, your country or region as the App Store reports it, your device model and iOS version, and a random ID Leanrrr makes up for this purpose. It contains nothing you log in Leanrrr and nothing that names you. RevenueCat doesn't use it for advertising. If you never buy or restore Even Leanrrr, RevenueCat receives nothing beyond that random ID and your device details when the app starts.

## Other services

Leanrrr doesn't sell or share your personal data, and it uses no analytics, advertising or tracking services. The only outside services it contacts are:

- **RevenueCat** (United States): receives the purchase records described under Purchases, and handles them under RevenueCat's privacy policy.

- **Apple** (United States and elsewhere): Maps, iCloud (including the public database that holds shared foods), Health, Siri, Visual Intelligence and the App Store (for Even Leanrrr), which are part of your device and your Apple Account and are covered by Apple's privacy policy.
- **Open Food Facts** (France): receives only a product's barcode number or the words you search for packaged foods. Neither is linked to you.
- **Recipe websites you choose**: when you import a recipe from a link, that website receives the request for the page and nothing else.
- **Supabase** (United States company; the data is stored in Sydney, Australia): hosts Leanrrr's food data (restaurant menus, everyday foods, supermarket foods and US packaged foods by barcode). A few times a day Leanrrr asks it whether that data has changed and, if it has, downloads the new copy; when you scan a barcode, that number is looked up there; suggestions you send are stored there. The requests contain nothing about you or your data beyond the suggestion itself. As with any website, Supabase sees your device's IP address and handles it under Supabase's privacy policy.

Any service that receives data from Leanrrr must protect it at least as well as this policy does. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated before the app sends anything new.

## Consumer health data

Leanrrr has a separate consumer health data privacy policy for Washington's My Health My Data Act and similar US state laws. It's in Settings → Privacy and legal → Consumer health data, and it covers the same data as this policy.

## Security

Your data is protected by your device's encryption and your Apple Account. Leanrrr only connects to other services over HTTPS.

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app with a new effective date.
